## Who Lahiru is

Lahiru Anuradha Dissanayake is a Data Platform Engineer and MLOps consultant based in Hildesheim, Lower Saxony, Germany. He has more than five and a half years of industry experience operationalising enterprise-scale machine learning systems on AWS and Azure. His GitHub handle is Data-Fenix and his personal brand is "Data Fenix".

Since 1 November 2026 he works as a Data Platform Engineer at Memodo GmbH in Germany. Before that he was a Working Student in Data and Machine Learning Engineering at Memodo, a Consultant and MLOps Engineer in the AI Factory of Dialog Axiata PLC in Sri Lanka, and a DevOps engineer for Exazyme and Fcode Labs.
