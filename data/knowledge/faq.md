His master's thesis applies attention-based deep reinforcement learning to the time-dependent travelling salesman problem, finding routes up to 6.8% better with about 97,000 times faster inference than traditional solvers on Beijing traffic data. He also co-authored research at ISMLL, University of Hildesheim, on graph-based forecasting of asynchronous time series, improving accuracy by up to 10.48%.
