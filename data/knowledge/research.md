## Deep reinforcement learning for the time-dependent travelling salesman problem

Repositories: https://github.com/Data-Fenix/DRLSolver4DTSPEnhanced and https://github.com/Data-Fenix/DRL4TDTSP. The thesis is titled "Windowed Encoding and Phase-Aware Deep Reinforcement Learning for Deterministic Time-Dependent TSP" and was supervised by Prof. Dr. Dr. Lars Schmidt-Thieme and Tim Dernedde at the University of Hildesheim. Lahiru's master's research proposes an attention-based deep reinforcement learning architecture for the time-dependent travelling salesman problem (TDTSP), where travel times change with traffic over the day. The enhancements are step-aware decoding, cost-aware gating with heuristics such as nearest neighbour and linear time, and time-sliced traffic encoding. Using real Beijing traffic data, the model finds solutions up to 6.8% better than baselines with approximately 97,000 times faster inference than traditional solvers. Training uses REINFORCE with a rollout baseline in PyTorch, with experiments on 19-customer instances and lambda sweeps for the heuristic weight. The code builds on the attention-learn-to-route model (Kool et al.) and the IEEE TNNLS paper "Solving Dynamic Traveling Salesman Problems With Deep Reinforcement Learning" by Zhang et al.

## Graphs for forecasting asynchronous time series

Co-authored research at ISMLL (Information Systems and Machine Learning Lab), University of Hildesheim, on graph-based models for forecasting asynchronous time series. The approach improved forecast accuracy by up to 10.48% on real-world ICU and climate datasets. The paper is available at https://github.com/Data-Fenix/GRAFITI/blob/main/Graphs_for_Asynchronous_Time_Series_Forecasting.pdf and the repository is https://github.com/Data-Fenix/GRAFITI.

## Home broadband data exhaustion prediction (applied research)

Applied research during Lahiru's data analyst role at Dialog Axiata PLC (2020 to 2021). He built a machine learning model with Oracle SQL and Python that predicts which HBB (home broadband) postpaid customers will exhaust their data within the next 7 days, so the business can reach them proactively. The model delivered a benefit of LKR 2 million through proactive management.
